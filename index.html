What we do

Every service has three layers: what comes in, what the agent prepares, what you decide.

How it works: Human Agency Layer

AI drafts, a person decides. At Pronto all the agency stays with people, and this rule is built into how every system works:

sales bot

When the sales bot doesn't know an answer, it tells the customer a manager will check.

our HQ

In our own HQ, no message or file goes out until a person approves it. The agents prepare three options, and a person picks one.

paid generation

Paid generation waits for an estimate and the owner's button. It never starts on its own.

your AI key

The AI key sits on your account, so you see the spend and can stop it.
